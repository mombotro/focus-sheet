Focus Sheet Maker

Build a copy-the-symbol focus sheet: 8 symbols, each with a blank box underneath to copy into. All the cells are the same size, and there's no title or name line — just the instruction and the grid. Prints as one US Letter sheet, landscape.

Controls

Generate 8 new invents eight brand-new symbols and puts them on the sheet. Each one is assembled from simple parts — shapes, lines, dividers and end-markers — combined by recipe, so they stay neat and copyable rather than random scribbles. Every click gives you a different set, at the level picked in "Level" above.

Export as PDF: the button opens the print dialog — choose Save as PDF, paper Letter, orientation Landscape, margins None, scale 100%. The page is already set to Letter landscape, so it should come out as a single 11 × 8.5 in page.
